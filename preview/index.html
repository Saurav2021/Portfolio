AURAAn Interpretable Stalking Score for Women's Safety Surveillance within an Explainable Four-Stage Video Pipeline

Saurav Kumar (first author) et al., 2026

Following someone is the behaviour that most often comes before harassment in public spaces, yet I couldn't find a system that measures it. AURA scores every pair of tracked pedestrians with five components an operator can read one by one, inside a pipeline where every alert carries SHAP attributions back to the evidence that raised it.

  • 87.0%violence-recognition accuracy on RWF-2000 (AUC 0.946), R(2+1)D-18 with BiLSTM attention
  • 30.5frames per second for detection and tracking on a single NVIDIA T4
  • 0.576mAP@50 for YOLOv11s detection on a disjoint validation split
  • 121false alerts on MOT17 footage with no stalking, reported and traced to three properties of the score instead of hidden
Under review after major revision, Journal of Ambient Intelligence and Humanized Computing (Springer)

SCRBMSemantic-Collaborative Restricted Boltzmann Machine for Recommender Systems: Architecture, Graph-Enhanced Extension, and Cross-Domain Evaluation

Saurav Kumar, Ayush, Sanjana Verma, Govind Kumar Jha, 2026

New users and sparse data break most recommenders. SCRBM conditions the hidden units of a Restricted Boltzmann Machine directly on Sentence-BERT item embeddings inside one energy function, and trains it with a ranking loss. SCRBM+ adds graph-smoothed user profiles and a density-adaptive gate. No language model runs at inference, so it stays cheap to serve.

Under review, Springer

SecSDAEA Semantic-Aware Contrastive Sequential Denoising Autoencoder for Cross-Modal Recommendation

Saurav Kumar, Govind Kumar Jha, 2026

Most sequential recommenders treat items as anonymous IDs and throw away what they are. SecSDAE reads each user's history twice, once through learned IDs and once through frozen Sentence-BERT text, aligns the two views with a cross-modal InfoNCE loss, and blends them with a position-wise gate that was never told how to balance them.

  • 0.8218HR@10 on MovieLens-1M (± 0.0030), best of 11 baselines on all 4 datasets
  • 12.84milliseconds for single-user inference on a CPU
  • 96.3%of HR@10 kept with only five history items; 96.6% under 40% noise

I report where it breaks

AURA's stalking score raised 121 false alerts on ordinary crowds, and the paper diagnoses why. SecSDAE's limitations name where it loses, including cold items, where it trails CL4SRec by 2.7% HR@10.

I check that a win is real

Five independent seeds, paired t-tests and effect sizes. SCRBM's lead over LightGCN holds at p < 0.0001 with Cohen's d = 9.06, and I rebuilt baselines under matched protocols.

I build inside real limits

Every experiment ran on free Kaggle T4 and P100 GPUs within 12-hour sessions, so checkpointing and fixed seeds come first, not last.
